Features

More than a travel tracker.

Imprint goes beyond pinning locations — it gives you a living, breathing map of your life's journey.

🗺️

Coverage Map

See exactly what percentage of the world, each country, and each region you've explored — visualized as a heat map that grows with you.

📍

Passive Tracking

Imprint quietly logs your location in the background. Just live your life — your map builds itself automatically.

👥

Friend Comparisons

See how your coverage stacks up against friends. Discover places they've been that you haven't — and challenge them to catch up.

🏆

Achievements & Milestones

Unlock badges for hitting every continent, exploring all 50 US states, visiting UNESCO World Heritage Sites, and hundreds more.

✈️

Trip Recaps

Every trip automatically becomes a shareable recap — a beautiful visual story of where you went, how far you traveled, and what you covered.

🎯

Bucket List

Pin places you want to visit next. Imprint will help you plan the most efficient route to maximize your coverage on your next adventure.
